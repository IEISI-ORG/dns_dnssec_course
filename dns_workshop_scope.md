# Advanced DNS/DNSSEC Workshop - Scope of Work Summary

A one-page summary. Program facts (length, hours, schedule, delivery options, assessment timing) are defined in the **Program Facts** section of `dns_dnssec_workshop_plan.md`. If this page and the plan disagree, the plan is correct.

## Executive Overview
The Advanced DNS/DNSSEC Workshop is a hands-on technical training program covering DNS fundamentals, DNSSEC, DNS privacy, registry operations (EPP and CoCCA), monitoring, and emerging technologies. It runs as 12 modules over six full days plus a Day 7 morning for the Final Project.

## Program Objectives
- **Build DNS Competency:** From basic concepts to advanced operational expertise
- **Enhance Security Skills:** DNSSEC implementation and DNS privacy technologies
- **Develop Registry Knowledge:** EPP protocol and CoCCA backend operations
- **Foster Best Practices:** Standard operational procedures and security
- **Prepare for Future:** Emerging technologies and architectural considerations

## Workshop Structure
- **Format:** Each module is 90 minutes of theory plus 90 minutes of lab, two modules per day. See the plan for the daily schedule and contact hours.
- **Modules:** listed with objectives, content and labs in `dns_dnssec_workshop_plan.md`
- **Slides:** outlined per module in `dns_slide_deck_plan.md`
- **Assessment:** daily graded labs, knowledge checks and a team Final Project; see `dns_assessment_plan.md`

## Delivery Options
Option letters, days and modules follow the plan:
- **Option A – Basic DNS Workshop:** network and system administrators new to DNS
- **Option B – Advanced Registry Operations:** registry and registrar technical staff
- **Option C – DNS Security Intensive:** security professionals and DNS operators

## Technical Requirements
- **Lab environment:** isolated network with internet gateway, multiple VMs per participant, snapshot reset (details in the plan's Appendix A)
- **Software:** BIND, Unbound, PowerDNS, CoCCA, monitoring tools; CoCCA access terms to be confirmed with CoCCA
- **Participants:** WiFi-capable laptop with SSH client; prerequisites as listed in the plan

## Deliverables
**For participants:** instructor-led training with hands-on labs, lab guides and reference material, and an IEISI course certificate on meeting the assessment plan's criteria (certificate scheme proposed).

**Documentation package:**
- Curriculum guide and lab manual: `dns_dnssec_workshop_plan.md`
- Slide outlines: `dns_slide_deck_plan.md`
- Assessment plan: `dns_assessment_plan.md`
- Slide decks: `modules/`

## Resource Requirements
- **Instructors:** at least 5 years of DNS operations, DNSSEC and registry experience, and training experience (full list in the plan)
- **Support:** lab coordinators, assessment administration, materials management
- **Hardware:** VM hosts, networking equipment, backup systems

## Implementation Timeline
1. **Development (3-4 months):** finalise curriculum, build and test labs, create assessments, train instructors, produce materials
2. **Pilot (1-2 months):** limited pilot workshops, feedback, refinement
3. **Full deployment:** regular delivery calendar, instructor development, partnerships

## Budget Considerations
- **Development costs:** curriculum development, lab infrastructure, assessment development, instructor training, materials
- **Operational costs:** instructor fees, infrastructure maintenance, software licensing, certificate administration, updates
- **Revenue opportunities:** workshop fees, corporate training, licensing agreements, partnership revenue, consulting services
